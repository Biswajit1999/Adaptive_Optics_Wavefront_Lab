01 / Starting point

Why I wanted to study this

Adaptive optics connects the subjects I want to understand together: astronomical instrumentation, wavefront sensing, and feedback control.

My aim was to build intuition from real telemetry while learning how an optical model and controller behave. Working through a public AOT dataset gave me a practical route from file formats and sensor streams to numerical validation and the limits of a claim.

The project’s contribution is the software and audit built around an existing release. The observations, instrument, and AOT standard belong to their original teams.

03 / Measured outcome

What survived the reduction?

All 79,200 retained telemetry values, 300 timestamps, and 300 source indices pass the declared parity tolerance. The three scalar diagnostics also pass the study’s mean and percentile thresholds.

Each diagnostic exceeds the 5% temporal acceptance limit. These are study-specific thresholds, not universal AO standards. A gate failure here limits the browser product; it does not indicate a fault in the telescope.

04 / Learning outcomes

What the work helped me understand

Data engineering

A plot needs a chain of custody.

FITS extension shapes, source-frame indices, timestamps, hashing, and rounding tolerances turn a visual demo into an inspectable data product.

Scientific reasoning

Different claims need different tests.

A sample can reproduce a distribution and still lose temporal content. Numerical integrity, representativeness, and physical interpretation require separate evidence.

Optics and control

A model’s assumptions are part of its result.

Noll normalization, sampled-pupil FFTs, delay, noise, and the exact feedback recurrence determine what the model can teach. Hardware calibration is still required for telescope inference.

05 / Next research question

A concrete next research question

How should a compact telemetry product be designed for a specified scientific use?

My proposed next experiment compares the current point sample with an explicitly filtered reduction and a full-rate reference. I would define the target bandwidth, filter response, edge handling, and acceptance tests before running the comparison. This is proposed work.
